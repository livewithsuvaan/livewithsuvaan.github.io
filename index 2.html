Private Event — Terms & Conditions

  • Minimum booking duration is 2 hours.
  • Full payment can be made immediately after the show.
  • Sound hire is charged separately from the performance fee.
  • If you have your own sound setup, only performance charges apply.
  • Additional performance time will incur extra charges.
  • Travel charges may apply outside Perth.
  • The client must provide a suitable performance area and power supply.
  • The client is responsible for any damage to my sound equipment caused by guests during performances or breaks.
  • Cancellations or date changes must be communicated in advance.
